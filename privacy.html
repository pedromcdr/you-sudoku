Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 2025

Your privacy is important to us. This policy explains what information we collect (very little), how we use it, and your rights.

🔒 Short version: YouSudoku does not collect any personal information. We do not use tracking cookies, we do not run ads, and we do not sell any data. You can play completely anonymously.

1. Information We Collect

YouSudoku is designed to work entirely in your browser. We do not require registration, login, or any personal information to play.

We do not collect:

  • Names, email addresses, or contact details
  • Location data or IP addresses stored long-term
  • Browsing history or behavioural data
  • Payment or financial information

Your language preference may be stored in your browser's URL parameters (e.g. ?lang=fr), which never leaves your device.

2. Cookies

YouSudoku does not use tracking cookies, advertising cookies, or any third-party analytics cookies.

Your browser may store technical data locally (such as game state via localStorage) solely to improve your experience. This data stays on your device and is never transmitted to us.

3. Third-Party Services

YouSudoku uses Google Fonts to display typography. When your browser loads the page, it contacts Google's servers to download fonts. This is subject to Google's Privacy Policy.

We do not use any advertising networks, social media trackers, or analytics platforms (such as Google Analytics).

4. Children's Privacy

YouSudoku is a safe, family-friendly game suitable for all ages. We do not knowingly collect any information from children under 13. Since we collect no personal data at all, no special measures are required.

5. Your Rights

Since we do not collect or store any personal data, there is nothing to access, correct, or delete. However, if you have any privacy concerns, please contact us and we will respond promptly.

6. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will update the date at the top of this page. Continued use of YouSudoku after changes means you accept the updated policy.

7. Contact

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us:
