About YouSudoku

We believe that great puzzles should be free, fast, and frustration-free. YouSudoku was built with one goal in mind: to give everyone on the planet access to a clean, distraction-free Sudoku experience.

Who We Are

YouSudoku is an independent puzzle platform created by a small team of developers and puzzle enthusiasts. We have no venture funding, no advertising partners, and no hidden agenda — just a genuine love for logic puzzles and clean design.

We launched YouSudoku because we were frustrated with Sudoku sites cluttered with ads, pop-ups, and mandatory sign-ups. We wanted something better — so we built it.

Our Mission

Our mission is simple: make the world's best free Sudoku game, accessible in as many languages as possible, on every device. No paywalls, no accounts, no data harvesting — just puzzles.

Every puzzle on YouSudoku is computer-generated with a unique solution and tested for quality at each difficulty level. Easy, Medium, and Hard — all solvable through pure logic.
